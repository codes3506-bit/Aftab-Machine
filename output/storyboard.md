# Storyboard: Khabri-1 Hill Post, Track Options (v3, 2:52, B3-Haryaan look)

- **Format:** 2560×1440 native at 30 fps, delivered as 1440p, 1080p and 720p (phone).
- **Content:** all five options get equal treatment. There is no recommendation, ranking or weighted score.

**Look**
- Same visual language as the B3–Haryaan film:
  - navy top bar with a gold rule;
  - navy panels with a gold glow;
  - Carlito type;
  - coloured label boxes;
  - live CH / ELEV / GRADE HUD at top right;
  - elevation profile strip at bottom left, filled by grade class (≤1:10 blue, 1:10–1:7 amber, >1:7 red).
- Terrain uses Esri Wayback 15 Aug 2024 imagery on Cop GLO-30 at VE ×1.5.
