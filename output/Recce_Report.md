**<u>RECCE REPORT - TR TO KHABRI-1 HILL POST (RL 133) - OPTIONS FOR DECISION</u>**

1. **<u>Gen</u>**. **Khabri-1 hill post** (25°25'38.6"N 63°26'23.2"E, RL 133 m) is located on the SW nose of **Taloband Rg**, above the **Shadi Kaur**. An existing tr runs from **CHW** to the foot of the old climb at CH 3.92 km. A new tr CH 3.92 - 5.23 km and a kacha tr CH 5.23 - 5.92 km have been planned to the plain pad (KMZ Opt 1 rev). In continuation, a desk study (terrain analysis) of all feasible alns from the existing/ planned tr to the **hill post** has been carried out.

2. **<u>Aim</u>**. To put fwd the desk study report for the tr to Khabri-1 hill post alongwith analysis of **5 x alns**, req of POL/ work days, and the options for decision.

3. **<u>Seq</u>**. This report shall unfold in fol seq: -
   a. Methodology.
   b. Terrain.
   c. Alns Consd.
   d. Comparison and Analysis.
   e. POL and Work Days.
   f. Maj Findings and Options for Decision.

4. **<u>Methodology</u>**. The study was carried out on Cop GLO-30 DEM (30 m), Esri imagery (0.6 m), the user KMZ, drone footage (848x480, no telemetry) and recce photographs. Alns were derived by least-cost analysis (grade limit 1:7, side-hill and N penalties), giving 5 x distinct approaches to the post. Fol were the key consds: -
   a. Gradient against the permissible 1:10 (ruling) and 1:7 (max pitch).
   b. Cut/ fill, N xings, slope stability and maint liability.
   c. Exposure to dominating ground and to W/ NW low gr (OPs assumed, ***to be confirmed***).

5. **<u>Terrain</u>**. Salient are as fol: -
   a. <u>Ridge</u>. The post crest is narrow, aligned ENE - WSW. Both faces are steep: avg **22°** (S) and **23°** (N) within 250 m of the post. The N face drops about 60 m to a strike hollow about 205 m N (DSM about 84 m).
   b. <u>Strata</u>. Bedding strikes ENE - WSW and crops out on both faces (drone 01:03, S face; recce oblique, N side). Beds are assessed gently dipping/ near-horizontal. No dip-slope face has been identified ***(to be confirmed by gr recce)***.
   c. <u>Drainage</u>. Nalas drain both flanks; the N foothill is crossed by 6 x nalas with catchment > 5 ha.
   d. <u>Existing tr to post</u>. No veh tr to the post is visible on imagery. A faint footpath runs W along the ridge spine (unconfirmed).

8. **<u>Maj Findings</u>**. After carrying out the desk study, fol are the maj findings: -
   a. <u>Rear aln exists</u>. A graded aln skirting the mount to the rear, running N along the foothill and climbing to the post from the N (T5) has been found by least-cost analysis and is corroborated by the DEM section and imagery.
   b. <u>Direct climb</u>. T1 is **not tech feasible** (30.0 % max grade).
   c. <u>Graded alns (T2 - T5)</u>. All four are tech feasible on desk study. Each has a different balance of grade, length, N xings, cut and exposure (para 6); no single aln is best on every count.
   d. <u>Accuracy</u>. Figures are based on a 30 m DEM; cut qty are assessed (± 50 %) ***(to be confirmed by gr recce)***.

9. **<u>Options for Decision</u>**. In view of the above, fol options are put fwd for decision, in no order of preference: -
   a. <u>T2 (graded S spur)</u>. Least POL and work days; 6 x hairpins; high exposure to W/ NW low gr.
   b. <u>T3 (W spur/ ridge)</u>. Uses the KMZ new tr; highest exposure to the crests.
   c. <u>T4 (E flank)</u>. Shortest graded aln; nil N xings; 8 x hairpins; 50 % exposure to the crests.
   d. <u>T5 (rear/ N foothill)</u>. Gentlest grades and least crest exposure of the graded alns (34 %); longest aln with 6 x N xings, which will need protection (Irish xings/ causeways).
   e. <u>T1 (direct climb)</u>. Listed for completeness; **not tech feasible** for veh mov.
   f. <u>Gr Recce</u>. Whichever aln is selected, gr recce is req before pegging (dip of strata, N xing sites, OP sites).
